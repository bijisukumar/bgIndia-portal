# StayVibe — What it does for a villa host

Written for a host evaluating the product, and structured so it can later feed
an in-app help section and a self-service bot. Every item below is **built and
running on dwarka.stayvibe360.com today** — nothing here is roadmap.

Four areas. Compliance leads because it is the one a host cannot solve with a
spreadsheet, and the one that carries a legal penalty.

---

## 1. Compliance — the part you legally cannot get wrong

Kerala hosts sit under the **Registration of Foreigners Act** and the **Kerala
Police Hotel Guest Rules**. Most small hosts handle this with a paper register
or a WhatsApp photo of a passport. That is the exposure StayVibe removes.

| What you get | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Digital check-in link**, one per booking channel | Guest fills it before arrival. No register, no queue at the door. |
| **Form C per foreign national** — not per booking | The law requires a separate Form C for *every* foreign guest, including children with their own passport. The form collects each one, with their own passport, visa and arrival details. |
| **Passport scan reading** | The guest photographs the passport page; the machine-readable zone is read and the fields pre-filled. They confirm — it never overwrites what they typed. |
| **Passport and visa images stored per guest** | Each scan is attached to the person it belongs to, not dumped against the booking. |
| **Indian guests take a separate track** | Aadhaar / PAN / licence instead. The two flows are kept strictly apart, so a domestic booking never ends up carrying foreign paperwork. |
| **"I'll bring originals at check-in"** | For guests who don't have documents to hand, without blocking their registration at midnight. |

Your staff see, per booking, which guests still have no passport on file.

## 2. Business development — filling the calendar, and keeping more of it

| What you get | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Enquiry pipeline** | Every enquiry logged, quoted, chased and marked won or lost. A conversion dashboard shows where they die. |
| **Channel calendar** | Pulls the iCal feeds from your OTA listings into one month grid, so an Airbnb booking and a Booking.com booking on the same night are visible before they collide. |
| **Double-booking detection** | If a check-in form arrives that overlaps an existing stay, the booking is flagged for review and you are alerted — rather than discovered on arrival day. |
| **Travel agent quote links** | Send an agent a link with your rates. Their quote comes back into the same pipeline. |
| **Campaign tracking links** | Create a named link per campaign or channel, then see clicks → enquiries → bookings against it. You learn which WhatsApp group or listing actually produces business. |
| **"Family needing flexibility" page** | A public page where a guest with an OTA booking asks for early check-in or late check-out. It quotes them, and puts a direct-booking offer in front of a guest who is currently paying OTA commission. This is your OTA-to-direct conversion route. |
| **Guest repository** | Every past guest, their stays and their contact details, so a repeat or referral is one search away. |

## 3. Brand & reviews — the thing that compounds

| What you get | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Guest messages, pre-written, one tap** | Arrival directions, a mid-stay comfort check, a host introduction, and a farewell note. You tap; WhatsApp opens with the message ready. Personal, but not from scratch each time. |
| **Review capture from Airbnb** | New reviews are picked up automatically, matched to the stay, and the rating and highlights recorded against it. |
| **Review chasing** | Stays that haven't produced a review are tracked so you can follow up deliberately instead of by memory. |
| **A check-in experience that looks like a business** | Your name, your villa, your branding — not a Google Form. It is often a guest's first interaction with you. |

---

## 4. Operational efficiency — the daily grind

| What you get | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Staff daily screen** | Your caretaker opens one link and sees today's active guest, the next five arrivals, and check-in/check-out **times** — plus a warning when a departure and an arrival share a day. |
| **Booking completion & money** | Per-booking tariff, OTA fees, extra charges, occupancy tax and what you actually net. OTA fee structures are built in. |
| **Expenses and incidentals** | Villa expenses, kitchen incidentals, breakfast and car-rental entries, logged by staff as they happen. |
| **Inventory and preferred stock** | What's in the villa, what to reorder. |
| **Staff & access** | Add staff, set what they can see, and record salary or salary-plus-commission. Check-in and check-out actions are recorded against the person who did them. |
| **No-show and refunds** | Mark a no-show and record a manual refund against the booking, so the accounts still reconcile. |
| **Notifications** | Choose what you get alerted about. |

## What a host should expect in the soft-launch

- **This is early.** You are getting it at ₹500 against a ₹2,000/month list
  price because your feedback shapes what gets built next.
- **Your data is yours and is kept separate from other hosts'.**
- **Setup is done for you** during onboarding — property details, rates, staff,
  branding, and your check-in links.
- **What is not automated:** guest messages open WhatsApp pre-written for you to
  send — the product does not send messages on your behalf, and does not run or
  spend money on advertising.
