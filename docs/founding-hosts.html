Guest registration that satisfies the law

Under the Registration of Foreigners Act and the Kerala Police Hotel Guest Rules, every foreign guest must be registered. Most of us do this with a paper register and a photo of a passport on a phone. This is the part StayVibe was built around first.

A check-in link per booking channel
The guest completes registration before they arrive. Nothing to fill in at your gate.
A separate Form C for every foreign guest
The law asks for one per foreign national, not one per booking — including children travelling on their own passport. The form collects each person's passport, visa and arrival details separately.
The passport photo page reads itself
Your guest photographs the page and the fields fill in. They confirm before submitting, so a bad scan never silently becomes bad data.
Indian guests follow their own track
Aadhaar, PAN or licence instead — and the two paths are kept strictly apart, so a domestic booking never picks up foreign paperwork it should not have.

More bookings, and more of them direct

Every OTA night costs you commission. The aim is to fill the calendar, then slowly move the guests you already have onto direct terms.

Enquiries that do not go cold
Every enquiry logged, quoted and chased, with a dashboard showing where they are being lost.
One calendar across your channels
Your OTA listings' calendars pulled into a single month view, so a clash between two platforms is visible before it becomes an argument at the door.
Double bookings flagged, not discovered
If a registration arrives that overlaps an existing stay, it is held for review and you are alerted.
Tracked links for each campaign
Share a different link per WhatsApp group, listing or agent, then see which ones actually turn into clicks, enquiries and bookings.
A page that turns OTA guests into direct ones
Guests asking for early check-in or a late check-out get a quote — and a reason to book with you directly next time.
Every past guest, kept
Their stays, their contacts, their preferences. Repeat guests are the cheapest bookings you will ever get.

Reviews and reputation, handled deliberately

Ratings compound. A host who asks well, at the right moment, out-earns a host who hopes.

Guest messages already written
Arrival directions, a mid-stay check, an introduction, a farewell note. One tap opens WhatsApp with the message ready — personal, without starting from a blank box each time.
Airbnb reviews collected on their own
New reviews are matched to the stay and the rating recorded, so you are not updating a spreadsheet by hand.
Stays with no review are tracked
So the follow-up is a decision, not something you remember three weeks late.
A first impression that looks like a business
Your villa's name and branding on the registration page, rather than a bare Google Form.

The daily running of the place

Most of the work is not booking guests. It is knowing who arrives at what time, what the villa is short of, and where the money went.

One screen for your caretaker
Today's guest, the next five arrivals, and the actual check-in and check-out times — with a warning when someone leaves and someone else arrives the same day.
What you actually earned
Tariff, OTA fees, extra charges, occupancy tax and your net, per booking. The channel fee structures are already built in.
Expenses logged where they happen
Villa expenses, kitchen incidentals, breakfast and car hire, entered by staff as they occur rather than reconstructed later.
Stock and reordering
What is in the villa and what needs buying before the next arrival.
Staff, access and pay
Add your people, control what each can see, and record salary or salary-plus-commission. Check-ins and check-outs are recorded against whoever did them.
No-shows and refunds
Recorded properly, so the month still reconciles.
